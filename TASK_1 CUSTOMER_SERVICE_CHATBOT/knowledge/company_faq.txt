# Customer Service Knowledge Base

## Company Overview

Our company provides online products and customer support services.
Customer support is available to help customers with orders, payments,
returns, refunds, and general product-related questions.

## Customer Support

Support Hours:
Monday to Friday: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST
Saturday: 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM IST
Sunday: Closed

Customers can contact customer support during the above support hours.

## Order Delivery

Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 7 business days after an order
has been shipped.

Delivery times may vary depending on the customer's location,
shipping conditions, holidays, and other unforeseen circumstances.

Customers can request an order status or tracking update from customer
support.

The chatbot must not invent a delivery date for a specific order.

## Delayed Orders

If an order is delayed, the customer should first check the available
tracking information.

If tracking information is unavailable or the package is significantly
delayed, the customer should contact customer support for assistance.

The chatbot must not claim to know the current location of an order
unless order-tracking information is available.

## Payments

Supported payment methods:

* Credit cards
* Debit cards
* UPI
* Net banking
* Selected digital wallets

Payment availability may depend on the customer's location and
checkout configuration.

The chatbot must not claim that a specific payment method is available
for a customer's account unless that information is provided.

## Returns

Eligible products can be returned within 7 days of delivery.

Products must meet the applicable return conditions.

Certain products may not be eligible for return.

Customers should contact customer support if they are unsure whether
their product qualifies for a return.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued after the returned product has been received and
the return has been approved.

Approved refunds are generally processed within 5 to 7 business days.

The actual time for the refund to appear in the customer's account may
depend on the payment provider or bank.

The chatbot must not promise a refund before eligibility and approval
have been confirmed.

## Account Information

The chatbot must not request or expose sensitive information such as:

* Passwords
* OTPs
* Full payment card numbers
* CVV
* Authentication codes

The chatbot may request non-sensitive information when necessary for
support, but it should avoid requesting unnecessary personal information.

## Escalation

If the chatbot cannot resolve a customer's issue using the available
information, it should explain that the issue may require assistance
from a human support representative.

The chatbot must not claim that a human agent has already reviewed
the customer's case unless that actually occurred.

## Important AI Rules

The chatbot must:

1. Use this knowledge base as the source of truth for company policies.
2. Never invent company policies.
3. Never invent order status or tracking information.
4. Never invent refund approval.
5. Never invent prices or discounts.
6. Clearly state when required information is unavailable.
7. Ask for clarification when necessary.
8. Maintain an appropriate tone based on detected customer sentiment.
